BEFORE YOU ORDER

Atelier notes.

Fit & sizing

Every product has its own size chart. Measurements are in inches. We recommend ordering one size up if you prefer more room. A 0.5–1 inch variation may occur.

Custom fit

Select custom fit during product selection if you want the piece tailored to your measurements. Custom pieces may have different exchange or cancellation terms.

Returns & exchanges

Please review the product-specific return and exchange terms before placing an order, especially for custom-made pieces.

Contact

For sizing, custom-fit or order questions, email contact@aliveraatelier.in.
